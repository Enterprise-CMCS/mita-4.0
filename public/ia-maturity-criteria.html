What the criteria cover

12 dimensions, 5 maturity levels

The IA maturity criteria are organized across 12 thematic dimensions and scored against 5 progressive maturity levels — from ad-hoc initial practices to fully optimized, continuously improving information management.

Criteria Dimensions

Information Quality Information Classification Information Analysis Information Exchange Information Reporting Information Content Information Metadata Information Governance Information Design Reference Information Master Information Information Storage

Maturity Levels

Level 1 Initial
Level 2 Developing
Level 3 Definition
Level 4 Managed
Level 5 Optimizing
