What is MITA 4.0?

A framework for Medicaid Enterprise Systems

The Medicaid Information Technology Architecture (MITA) 4.0 is a framework designed to help states and territories develop and implement enterprise-wide information technology systems that support Medicaid business processes. MITA 4.0 represents the latest evolution of this framework, incorporating modern approaches to enterprise architecture and outcomes-based planning.
